Less scrolling.

Ronumi is a calm focus timer and app blocker for Android, with a small companion who cheers you on.

Get it on GitHub Coming soon to Google Play

Focus with a friend

Pick a task, choose how long, and start. Ronumi keeps you company while the timer runs.

  • Timer, stopwatch, and Pomodoro with breaks
  • Calm animated scenes and focus sounds
  • Strict mode for sessions you cannot quit early

Block what pulls you away

Open a blocked app and Ronumi steps in. Your rules, on your schedule.

  • Block apps during focus or at set times
  • Daily limits for your most-used apps
  • Block websites and endless short-video feeds
  • Hold notifications until you are done

Progress you can see

Every session counts. Keep your streak alive, finish daily quests, and earn new clothes for Ronumi as you level up.

  • Streaks, levels, badges, and daily quests
  • Screen time and focus reports
  • A wardrobe full of outfits, hats, and colors

Your phone, your data

Ronumi needs no account. Focus, blocking, and reports work offline, and your history stays on your phone.

On your device

Settings, sessions, app usage, and held notifications never leave your phone. Backups are encrypted with your password.

Free and open source

Ronumi is free software under the GPLv3. The full source code is on GitHub.

Ronumi Plus

One optional payment on Google Play unlocks unlimited blocks, websites, strict mode, and more. No subscription. In the Play version, Plus also removes ads.
